QUICK GUIDE

A ray-traced game-lighting lab.

The viewport traces the Project-Drive ControlVehicle and studio geometry as indexed triangles through a per-mesh BVH. GI candidates are extended over multiple diffuse bounces, then selected and reused with spatiotemporal reservoirs.

Orbit cameraDrag over the viewport. Scroll to dolly.
Drive the carW / S throttle and brake/reverse, A / D steer, Space handbrake—or hold the viewport drive pad.
GTX compatibilitySoftware BVH compute is the portable fallback. Use the GTX preset for a lower ray and history budget.
RTX compatibilityRTX can run the same compute path at higher quality. A browser cannot expose DXR / Vulkan RT cores; native hardware RT needs a native host backend.
!

Not an SDF renderer. No signed-distance volumes, sphere tracing, or distance-field scene representation are used.
