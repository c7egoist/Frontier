Landmass Studio starts from a randomized base. Random landmass picks a new shape, seed, coverage, coast roughness, relief, terrain roughness, and ocean depth while preserving your world size and resolutions. World size changes the existing heightfield's real width in metres without erasing sculpt edits, up to a flat 40,075 km planet-sized plane; the viewport renders that physical field in a stable 2,400-unit scene and converts picking, brush, shoreline, and cell measurements back to metres. Landmass resolution rebuilds only the heightfield at 128²–512². The separate Water resolution control in Ocean rebuilds the 2.5D water grid at 128²–1024² and resamples the landmass bed into it. Generation replaces sculpt edits. This is only the broad land/ocean base—no erosion, rivers, snow, rocks, vegetation, or other systems.

EROSION BAKE — MESH → SDF 3D PROCESSES

The generated placement mesh is voxelized into a true signed-distance field, eroded in 3D, contoured again, and only then QEM-baked. World scale and grounded Y=0 are preserved. Enable Watch erosion live to see staged SDF checkpoints in the viewport while the final bake continues.

Set every process yourself above. Terrain Studio’s laws are adapted to overhangs and undersides: capacity-limited rain, gusting wrap-around wind saltation whose weather system persists in one sector, then veers or backs smoothly through intervening bearings to another while the chosen prevailing sector remains most common, repose-gated talus, freeze–thaw wedging, and wet-path chemical dissolution. All share hardness, sharp-edge and thin-span protection — incision, not melting. Live frames are previews only; export always uses the completed final mesh.

This uses the same virtual-pipe principle as Terrain Studio's 2.5D water. Incoming swell travels through the simulated depth/velocity field; foam is created where waves become depth-limited, run up the shore, or strike steeper ground. That whitewater quantity then moves with the water, spreads, and decays on a foam surface above the water. It is not a fragment-noise pattern and not particles.
