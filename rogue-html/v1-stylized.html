ROGUE // GOLD SWELL

Live HTML concept of the wave from 09-rogue-gold-swell — a spline silhouette extruded along its crest, dressed with shader foam, spray particles and a sunset rig.

drag = orbit  ·  scroll = zoom  ·  CHASE follows the lead car  ·  BARREL puts you inside the tube

THE WAVE, IN HTML

  • Silhouette spline — 11 control points trace the cross-section: steep face → crest → pitching lip.
  • Catmull-Rom smooths it, then it's extruded along the crest axis (160×181 grid ≈ 29k verts) with a subtle crest bow.
  • Shader patch on one Phong material: layered-sine vertex ripple + a foam mask (lip & base) × scrolling noise = moving whitewater.
  • Whitewater planes — two canvas-noise textures scrolling opposite directions at the trough.
  • Spray — two additive particle pools with gravity: tyre roost + lip spray.
  • Cars — parametric carve paths down the face, banking with the weave.
  • Golden hour — gradient sky-dome shader with sun-glow term, warm fog, backlit key light.
  • three.js r128 vendored locally — one page, no build, no CDN.
