REALISTIC OCEAN — THE TECH

  • GPU displacement — 14 Gerstner wave components summed in the vertex shader over a 212k-vertex radial-graded grid (dense near the wave, cheap at the horizon).
  • The rogue wave is a live field — a breathing envelope with a curling lip made via Gerstner-style horizontal shear; normals re-derived every frame by finite differences.
  • PBR-style water — Schlick Fresnel reflecting a procedural sunset sky, GGX sun glitter, sub-surface glow through backlit crests, foam from wave folding × scrolling FBM.
  • Image-based lighting — the sky is captured into a PMREM environment map; car paint is clearcoat PBR lit by it.
  • Post — ACES filmic tonemapping + hand-rolled HDR bloom chain (bright pass → separable blur → composite).
  • three.js r147 vendored — one page, no build, no CDN.
