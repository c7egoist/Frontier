Why this never goes silent at low FPS

  • Audio clock is the master. Web Audio runs on its own high-priority thread at 48 kHz. Oscillator & noise nodes are slewed via AudioParam.linearRamp against ctx.currentTime, not requestAnimationFrame.
  • Physics is fixed-step (240 Hz) on a wall-clock timer. An accumulator integrates performance.now() → 4.16 ms steps via setInterval / worker, independent of the render cap. Even at 5 FPS the tach, boost and loads evolve smoothly and audio still receives 240 updates/s.
  • Proof: set “Render cap” to 5 FPS — rAF draws 5×/s but the spectrum, boost gauge and turbo whine remain continuous. The headroom bar shows audio thread health; the physics chip stays ~240 Hz.
  • No sample library. Exhaust is additive: firing frequency = rpm/60 × cylinders/2 with per-vehicle harmonic weights (e.g. crossplane V8 has strong 0.5 & 1.5 sub-harmonics for burble; V12 is 6th/12th dominant). Turbo/SC are first-principles (boost lag τ, gear-whine frequency = rpm/60 × drive × lobes).

Secondary & induction

  • Turbo: 1st-order spool τ=0.32–0.48 s up, 0.11 s blow-off; anti-lag holds 0.38 bar at 4200+ rpm; BOV chirp on throttle drop >0.35 at >0.35 bar.
  • Supercharger: mechanical whine ∝ rpm (no lag), parasitic loss 38 Nm at redline, FM wobble at high boost.
  • Twincharged (Challenger): compound sum with 55/45 SC/turbo balance, 1.65 bar peak.
  • Secondary: overrun pops when throttle 0 >3400 rpm (≈1.8% per frame, filtered noise 950 Hz), transmission whine ∝ wheel speed, wind = bandpassed noise ∝ v¹·⁴⁵, limiter flutter cuts gain 58% randomly.
  • Electric (Taycan): inverter whine = polePairs × rotorHz + carrier FM 8.2–11 kHz, regen howl on brake, 2-speed shift at ~8500 motor rpm.

Driving

  • W/S throttle/brake (rates 7.0/6.5 s⁻¹). A/D steer adds subtle diff load (±4% cornering reserve). Q/E manual shift (auto also at 92% redline). Space neutral.
  • Torque curves interpolated smoothstep between dyno points; effective mass includes crank inertia I·G²/R² so high gears feel heavier — honest longitudinal dynamics.
