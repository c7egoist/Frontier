Method & scope

Landform presets establish cliff proportions as true SDF volumes. Ridged and cellular noise vary the mass on all sides, including overhangs past vertical. The same seeds reproduce the same geometry.

Stacks-style strata bands carry per-band hardness so soft beds retreat. Columnar or block joints, talus at the angle of repose, and crown gullies follow. Surface nets mesh the field; satmaps (slope, strata, cavity, flow) drive the grain material.

Seeded construction tool with staged rebuilds, not a physics simulation. Voxel resolution bounds the finest detail.
