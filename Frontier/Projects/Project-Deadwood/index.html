Iron Tide: stacked cars and shacks hide the next alley. Moving metal warns of an active crane magnet. Avoid the inner field: it can drag and lift your car. A buckling crane comes down with its load. Cargo drones hover over dark drop shadows before dumping. Light scrap can be pushed; heavy wrecks and fallen booms need another gap. R creates a fresh layout.

Icebound: look for scattered pale snow islands for grip—there is no marked route. Black ice preserves your sideways momentum. Brake before turning and keep below the displayed ice limit. Spreading cracks warn of a breakthrough. Do not stop for long: the floor falls away behind you. Ridges and open water split and reconnect the space. Random showers fall across snow and ice, not only at fixed stations. Watch the growing dark shadows and commit to a different gap before impact. Avalanches open fragile cuts. The final portal leads into the scrapyard in this same map.

Disturbed earth warns of an eruption. Roots erupt from scattered sites, vertically or at an angle, and fork from swollen nodes. Growth keeps spreading and thickening. Rocks and sand mark a fresh eruption. Slow down to place the car, but don't linger: a root front follows you.

In The Hunger: steer around the open flytrap mouths. Buried traps give a brief soil twitch, then erupt and lunge fast. Fireflies flash before exploding; reverse or clear the swarm. Blast damage persists until restart. Terrain rolls and stones collide. Being swallowed ends the attempt.
