The wall probe is the story. Before, its direct estimate was 0.000000 across all 64 frames — the reservoir locked onto an occluded sample and never escaped. The spatial-snapshot fix (patch 0016) clears it. That is the anomaly behind the DI = 0.0000 runs in the committed ReSTIRConvergence.txt, now caught at 5× the resolution.

The GI reconnection costs +28.7 % on the full pipeline, and +17.9 % of the frame is now the spatial pass alone (was 0.2 %). Each tap pays two bounce traces to re-evaluate the offered path at the receiver — that is what correct ReSTIR-GI reuse costs.

This retroactively justifies turning the GI pool off by default far more strongly than the original estimate. The pool was costing a second full ReSTIR pipeline for 0.6–1.6 % RMSE; it is now measurably ~29 % of frame time for that. On a GPU the bounces are BVH traversals rather than analytic intersections, so the constant will differ — but the shape will not.
