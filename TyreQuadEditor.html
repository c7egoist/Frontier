Tyre Quad Editor

Draw / import vector shapes → real all-quad tread mesh → arrayed & wrapped on the tyre. No heightmaps.

Tread presets shape-based

These are built from the same editable shapes you draw — including the asymmetric off-road and UHP patterns that a heightmap generator mangles. Load one, then look at its shape list.

Tyre

Array around tyre

One tile repeats around the circumference and is welded at the seam. Alternate-mirror / flip modes are how real alternating off-road patterns are made.

Quad meshing

Shape edges become mesh constraints; each region (lug top, groove floor, sipe) is meshed, split to quads and smoothed. Lower detail = finer, cleaner, slower.

Deformation test

This moves the actual quad vertices (contact-patch load, twist, inflation) — the reason you wanted clean quads instead of a displaced heightmap grid.

Export / import

OBJ faces are written as 4-vertex f a b c d — open it in Blender and check the topology yourself. (GLB/glTF would force triangles, so it is deliberately not offered as the "quad" export.)

Shape editor one tread tile

Drag a shape to move it, drag its white points to reshape. Pen: click to add points, double-click / Enter to close, Esc to cancel. The tile repeats above/below (around the tyre) and left/right is across the tread. Shapes may cross the tile edge — they wrap.
