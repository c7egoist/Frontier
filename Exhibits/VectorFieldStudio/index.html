Field Studio

Purpose

A GPU-resident vector field workspace for air, force, and terrain analysis. The particle advection, field slices, and glyph grid evaluate the same field function on the GPU; the inspector exposes the parameters and live statistics.

Field model

Wind combines a directional boundary flow, curl-noise turbulence, and terrain lift. Force sums attractors, repulsors, and vortices with a local-support falloff. Terrain follows the downhill gradient of the heightfield, with flow accumulation and vegetation sampled from the same terrain data.

Performance

The particle state lives in floating-point render targets and is advected by a fragment shader. Quality controls the particle texture size: 36k, 65k, or 147k particles. Rain uses a separate 16k state. The slice and glyphs evaluate the field directly, so no CPU readback is needed for the image.

Persistence

Scenes autosave to local storage. Use the save and open actions to move a scene as JSON. The renderer, field parameters, layer visibility, camera, and probe are included.
